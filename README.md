
# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes employee attrition and workforce composition to help HR teams identify retention risks and act on them.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- 📈 **Power BI Desktop** - data visualization
- 🧮 **DAX** - calculated measures (Attrition Rate %, Average Age, Average Experience, etc.)
- 🔄 **Power Query (M)** - data cleaning and transformation

## ✨ Highlights

### ❓ Business Problem
Employee attrition is costly and hard to manage without visibility into *where* and *why* it's happening. HR and leadership needed a single view to spot which departments, salary bands, roles, and demographics are driving turnover.

### 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard
Give HR and business stakeholders a self-serve, filterable view of headcount and attrition, so they can quickly spot high-risk segments (by department, job role, salary, age, gender, experience) and prioritize retention efforts.

### 🚶 KPI Walkthrough
- 👥 **Total Employees** - overall headcount
- ✅ **Active Employees** - employees currently with the company
- 🚪 **Attrition Count** - number of employees who left
- 📉 **Attrition Rate %** - attrition as a percentage of total headcount
- 🎂 **Average Age** - average age across the workforce
- ⏳ **Average Experience** - average tenure (years at company)

Supporting visuals break these KPIs down by:
- 🏢 **Department** (donut chart, funnel chart) — where attrition and headcount concentrate
- 💰 **Salary Slab** (bar chart) — whether pay band correlates with attrition
- 🧑‍💼 **Job Role & Job Satisfaction** (pivot table) — which roles/satisfaction levels see the most turnover
- 🎈 **Age Group** (column chart) — workforce age distribution
- ⚧️ **Gender** (pie chart) — attrition split by gender
- 📅 **Years of Experience** (area chart) — attrition trend as tenure increases
- 🎛️ Department and Age Group **slicers** let users filter the entire page interactively.

### 💡 Business Insight
- 🎯 Pinpoints the departments and salary bands with the highest attrition, enabling targeted retention budgets instead of blanket policies.
- 🌱 Highlights whether attrition is concentrated among newer or more tenured employees, informing onboarding vs. long-term retention strategy.
- ⚠️ Surfaces job roles with low satisfaction *and* high attrition, flagging where role redesign or manager intervention may help most.
